# Information Comparison Sample

## Task Overview

This sample demonstrates a basic information comparison workflow used in AI data verification and quality-control tasks.

The objective is to compare two pieces of information and identify whether they match or contain meaningful differences.

## Comparison Process

1. Review both pieces of information carefully.
2. Compare the relevant fields or attributes.
3. Identify matching information.
4. Identify any differences or discrepancies.
5. Determine whether the information matches according to the task guidelines.
6. Record the result clearly and consistently.

## Example

| Field | Information A | Information B | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product Name | Wireless Mouse | Wireless Mouse | Match |
| Brand | ExampleTech | ExampleTech | Match |
| Model | WM-2025 | WM-2025 | Match |
| Quantity | 5 | 5 | Match |
| Category | Computer Accessories | Electronics | Difference |

## Comparison Result

The two records contain mostly matching information, but the **Category** field is different.

This demonstrates the importance of comparing individual fields rather than assuming that two records are identical.

## Quality Checks

When comparing information, I focus on:

- Checking each relevant field
- Identifying exact differences
- Avoiding assumptions
- Distinguishing meaningful differences from formatting differences
- Recording results consistently
- Following project-specific instructions
